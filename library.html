Fat Loss & Metabolic

5-Amino-1MQ

An NNMT-inhibitor class compound, studied in metabolic research for its role in cellular NAD+ regulation and fat-cell energy metabolism.

AOD-9604

A modified fragment of human growth hormone (amino acids 176–191), studied in isolation from the rest of the GH molecule for effects on fat metabolism specifically.

Cagrilintide

A long-acting amylin analog, studied alongside GLP-1 agonist pathways for its role in satiety signaling.

MOTS-c

A mitochondrial-derived peptide encoded in mitochondrial DNA, studied for its role activating AMPK, a central regulator of cellular energy metabolism.

Retatrutide

A triple-agonist compound acting across GIP, GLP-1, and glucagon receptor pathways simultaneously, among the most extensively studied compounds in current metabolic research.

SS-31

A mitochondria-targeted peptide that concentrates in the inner mitochondrial membrane, studied in oxidative-stress and cellular-energy models.

Blends & Protocols

GLOW

A three-compound blend combining repair-focused peptides with a copper tripeptide, studied across skin, hair, and tissue-repair research models.

HHB

A nine-part B-vitamin and choline blend, formulated as a cofactor-support vial for methylation and metabolic-pathway research.

KLOW

A four-compound blend studied together in gut, joint, and skin inflammation research models.

Semax + Selank Blend

A combined vial pairing two Russian-developed neuropeptides, studied together for their overlapping effects on cognitive and anxiolytic pathways.

SHB

A nine-part amino-acid and NAC (N-acetylcysteine) blend, formulated for combined antioxidant and cofactor-pathway research.

Wolverine (BPC-157 + TB-500)

A pre-combined vial of two of the most widely studied repair peptides, packaged together for whole-body tissue-repair research.

Repair & Recovery

BPC-157

A synthetic fragment derived from a protein found in gastric juice. One of the most studied repair peptides, examined for its role in angiogenesis and tendon-healing signaling.

GHK-Cu

A naturally occurring copper tripeptide, studied for its role activating genes associated with tissue repair and collagen production.

KPV

A tripeptide fragment of alpha-MSH, studied for anti-inflammatory signaling independent of the parent hormone's other effects.

TB-500

A synthetic fragment of Thymosin Beta-4, studied for whole-body repair signaling and its role in cell migration and vascular formation.

Growth Hormone

CJC-1295 (No DAC) + Ipamorelin

A combined GHRH/GHRP protocol pairing a growth-hormone-releasing hormone analog with a selective secretagogue, studied together for pulsatile growth-hormone release patterns.

Ipamorelin

A selective growth-hormone-releasing peptide (GHRP), studied for stimulating GH release without the cortisol or prolactin elevation seen with earlier-generation secretagogues.

Sermorelin

A GHRH analog representing the first 29 amino acids of growth-hormone-releasing hormone — among the earliest secretagogues studied and used clinically.

Tesamorelin

A stabilized GHRH analog, studied specifically in visceral-fat research and one of the few compounds in this class with an established clinical research history.

Cognitive & Nootropic

DSIP

Delta Sleep-Inducing Peptide, first isolated from rabbit brain tissue, studied for its influence on slow-wave sleep architecture.

Selank

A synthetic analog of a naturally occurring immunomodulatory peptide, studied in Russian research for anxiolytic effects without the sedation associated with benzodiazepines.

Semax

A synthetic peptide derived from ACTH, studied for effects on focus, memory consolidation, and neuroprotective pathways in the central nervous system.

Longevity

Epithalon

A synthetic tetrapeptide studied for its proposed role in telomerase activation — among the most studied compounds in longevity-focused peptide research.

Pinealon

A short tripeptide studied in brain-targeted aging research, proposed to support neuronal regulatory function.

Thymosin Alpha-1

An immune-regulating peptide with a long research and clinical history, studied and approved under other names in numerous countries for immune-modulation research.

Sexual & Hormonal

HCG

Human Chorionic Gonadotropin, a glycoprotein hormone that mimics luteinizing hormone (LH), studied in testosterone-pathway and reproductive-endocrine research.

Kisspeptin-10

A fragment of the kisspeptin peptide, studied for its role as an upstream activator of the body's own LH/FSH release cascade.

Melanotan I

A selective melanocortin-1 receptor agonist, studied specifically in pigmentation research.

Melanotan II

A broader melanocortin receptor agonist affecting multiple receptor subtypes, and the parent compound from which PT-141 was derived.

PT-141

Bremelanotide, a melanocortin agonist studied for central-nervous-system pathways related to arousal, distinct from the vascular mechanism used in other approaches in this research area.

Aesthetic & Cosmetic

AHK-Cu

A copper tripeptide related to GHK-Cu, studied specifically in hair-follicle research.

SNAP-8

A topical octapeptide, studied for its effect on the muscle-contraction pathways linked to expression-line formation.

Supplies & Cofactors

Acetic Acid

A 0.6% sterile diluent used specifically for reconstituting IGF-1-family compounds, which are less stable in bacteriostatic water alone.

Bacteriostatic Water

Sterile water containing 0.9% benzyl alcohol as a preservative — the standard diluent for reconstituting most lyophilized research peptides.

Glutathione

The body's primary intracellular antioxidant, studied here in an injectable research format for redox and detoxification-pathway research.

Melatonin

The primary circadian-signaling hormone, prepared here as an injectable research-grade vial rather than the oral form more commonly seen.

NAD Buffer

A pH-buffered NAD+ formulation, developed to reduce the injection-site irritation associated with standard NAD+ solutions.

NAD+

A coenzyme central to cellular-energy metabolism across nearly every cell type, and a compound known to decline naturally with age — a major driver of current longevity research interest.

Vitamin B12

A methylation cofactor, studied alongside nerve-function and red-blood-cell-production pathways.
